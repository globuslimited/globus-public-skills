# Ads and assets

Promises come only from [claims](positioning-and-claims.md). Destinations come from [campaigns](campaigns-and-markets.md).

## RSA

- 15 headlines of 30 characters or fewer, 4 descriptions of 90 or fewer. Count and show every length, including spaces and punctuation.
- Pin the minimum-order headline in position 2. Pin the description that states the minimum and why to enquire in position 1. Delivery terms stay on the landing page.
- At least 2 headlines on the ad group's theme or keyword. At least 3 headlines from the allowed claims. At least 1 headline for designers or architects. At least 1 filtering call to action, such as Send Your Floor Plan.
- Each headline is a separate idea. At most two headlines share a theme. Do not repeat one thesis or a phrase such as One Team.
- Title Case for headlines, sentence case for descriptions, Globus for the company.
- No exclamation marks in headlines, and at most one in a description. No decorative symbols, emoji, repeated punctuation, or ALL CAPS except US, UK, QC, and FF&E. This is an editorial standard, not a claim that Google forbids each of these.
- Do not use outside brands, copy signals, cheap, affordable, discount, sale, deal, free, apartment, studio, small, names of single products, unproven superlatives, savings claims, prices other than the minimum order, duties, customs, tariffs, or "our factory" / manufacturer.

## Images

Default to real photos of finished projects, showrooms in use, production, and QC. Ask the operator where a file came from before attaching it. Names record the source: `_real`, `_aiedit`, `_aigen`. Example: `GLB_IMG_villa-living-room_real_20261101`.

Use 1.91:1 and 1:1, cropped from the original. Do not add overlaid text, logos, or graphics, collages, replaced backgrounds, or blurred or distorted photos. Outside logos, signs, and catalogs must not be visible. Do not use the business logo as the image asset.

AI generation or editing needs agreement. Do not present a fictional result as a real project, showroom, or factory. Check the current labeling rules before attaching generated media. If required labeling cannot be set, wait. Do not treat an old brief's statements about EU, India, or New York law as a legal conclusion.
