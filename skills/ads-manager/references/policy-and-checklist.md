# Policy limits and checklist

Company limits apply as project rules. A note in an old brief that something is a Google rule is not a check we have done. Before advising on a penalty, a trademark, AI labeling, a law, or a product capability, read the current primary source. If you cannot, say so.

Starting points named by the company source:

- [Counterfeit goods](https://support.google.com/adspolicy/answer/176017)
- [Trademarks](https://support.google.com/adspolicy/answer/6118)
- [Image asset requirements](https://support.google.com/adspolicy/answer/10347108)
- [Image quality](https://support.google.com/adspolicy/answer/9193529)
- [AI labeling](https://support.google.com/adspolicy/answer/17257106)

Do not repeat, without checking, claims about a lifetime ban, a ban spreading to a legal entity or a domain, a warning of exactly seven days, or a legal change on a named date.

## Internal safety limits

- Do not combine an outside brand with a copy signal anywhere in ad content. Do not use the signals themselves in positive keywords, copy, paths, names, or assets.
- The source list is: replica, dupe, copy, knockoff, knock off, clone, imitation, fake, faux, mirror, mirror image, lookalike, look-alike, inspired by, alternative to, similar to, style of, same as, 1:1, AAA. Include inflections and close wording.
- `[brand]-style` is also banned. Honest style words are allowed: Italian style, modern Italian design, European design, contemporary, minimalist, classic, luxury.
- Outside brands stay out of ad text, display paths, sitelinks, callouts, snippets, and the names of assets, campaigns, and ad groups. Retailers and marketplaces (IKEA, Wayfair, RH, Amazon, Alibaba, Made-in-China) are negatives only. The exception for agreed brand keywords is in [keywords](keywords.md).
- Do not present GLOBUS as a manufacturer or as a brand's official representative. Claims must match the landing page.
- Do not show Google one page and the user another. Do not work around a disapproval with spelling tricks, extra accounts, payment profiles, or domains. On a disapproval, name the policy and propose a fix. An appeal is the operator's decision.
- The limits apply to PAUSED objects. PAUSED does not mean the asset skipped review.

## Self-check

Check before a change is agreed and again after it is done, against the state you read. Mark PASS, FAIL, not checked, or not applicable. Missing data is not PASS. An unchecked critical safety item blocks that action. Check only what the task touches.

### A. Safety

- No copy signals, and no outside brand combined with one, in ad materials.
- Outside brands are absent from text, paths, names, and assets, unless a brand-keyword exception was agreed.
- Promises match [allowed claims](positioning-and-claims.md) and the landing page. No unproven savings, duties, or superlatives.
- Destinations are approved. No globus.world, shorteners, or cross-domain redirects.
- Image origin is confirmed. Reality, content, and any required AI label are satisfied.
- New objects are PAUSED. Nothing test or template was created.
- Shared negative lists were read. Banning copy signals in our materials does not require those queries to be negatives. Lists and their links are not changed automatically.

### B. Budget and settings

- The total and the split match the operator's current agreement, including every campaign in the launch.
- Type, networks, AI Max, auto-assets, and URL expansion match the [campaign standard](campaigns-and-markets.md) or an explicit exception.
- Meta `publisher_platforms` is only `instagram`.
- PRESENCE, regions, language, and landing page are right. Madrid is not in the English Spain plan.
- The schedule is all day, or the agreed window, and it was converted back to local time with daylight saving and the day shift.

### C. Keywords and materials

- Exact or phrase, whole-project intent, no single-product keywords.
- RSA: 15 headlines of 30 or fewer, 4 descriptions of 90 or fewer, lengths shown, minimum order pinned in the required positions.
- At least two theme headlines, three proof headlines, one designer headline, and one filtering call to action. Ideas are not repeated.
- Editorial rules, extension limits, links, and images match [ads](ads-and-assets.md).

### D. Measurement

- Primary and Secondary roles and the biddable goals were read. No unagreed role change.
- UTM, a successful form, duplicate risk, and gclid were checked as far as the task requires.
- Only the agreed plan was done. Changed entities were read again. Errors and anything unchecked are in the report.
