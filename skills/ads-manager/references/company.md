# Company, product, and buyers

## Product

- GLOBUS has worked since 2008 and is based in Foshan, Guangdong, China.
- Whole-project fit-out: furniture, lighting, bathrooms, and decor. Also 3D visualization and built-in furniture: kitchens, wardrobes, and casework.
- The service cycle is factory selection, drawing checks, production control, quality inspection, logistics, and help with customs paperwork.
- The company buys from vetted Chinese factories. Do not present it as a factory owner, a manufacturer, or an official dealer or distributor of another brand.
- Whole projects only, at least one container. Retail and single pieces are not sold.
- Minimum order US$30,000. A typical order is about US$80,000. The typical order is internal context, not an advertising price promise.
- First contact to contract is 6–18 months. The purchase waits until the property is ready for finishing.

## Qualification

A qualified lead is a real property, a whole project, and a budget from US$30,000. A cheap form without those signs is not success.

1. Owners of villas, houses, and large premium apartments, often 45+, with above-average income. They want a European, especially Italian, look without paying for a brand name.
2. Interior designers, architects, and developers buying for client projects. Repeat projects make this the more valuable segment.

Out of scope: one sofa, bed, or chair; cheap, ready-made, or used furniture; budget under the threshold; no property and no timing; wholesalers, dealers, resellers, and dropshippers; job seekers, students, and DIY; buyers of one outside brand or a copy of it.

The buyer's main fear is defects, wrong sizes, and an unreliable factory when ordering from China. The answer in communication is the company's own inspectors, a check before shipping, work since 2008, and clients in 55+ countries.
