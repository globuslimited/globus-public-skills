# Keywords and search terms

Who counts as a customer is in [company](company.md). Brand safety is in the [checklist](policy-and-checklist.md).

## Positives

Exact and phrase only. No broad. Each keyword must mean a whole project or buying through an agent. Use 5–20 closely related keywords in an ad group. The seed list is a pool to choose from, not an order to upload all of it.

Single products (sofa, bed, chair, table, lamp), even with "luxury", do not qualify. Do not use cheap, affordable, discount, sale, wholesale, near me, or the copy signals in the checklist. `apartment` and `manufacturer` need a separate decision and watching. Large premium apartments remain a target property type.

## Other brands

Do not propose these on your own. Only when the operator asks, and only with an explicit yes.

- A separate AG9_BrandTest. Exact. The brand is only in the keyword, never in ads, paths, names, or assets.
- The landing page has no outside brands and no copy language. Ordinary Globus copy.
- Do not combine a brand with a copy signal, even in exact match.
- Spend share at most 10% of that campaign's budget. This is a test condition, not a separate campaign budget. An ad group has no hard budget of its own. Show the operator how spend will be watched and limited.
- Review queries daily while the test runs. A trademark restriction, or a qualified-lead rate below the campaign average after four weeks, is a reason to propose pausing it under the approval rules.

Check the current Google trademark rules separately. The business risk is expensive queries for one original piece instead of a whole project.

## Negatives

Before creating or changing a campaign, read the live shared negative list and where it is applied. Propose missing words. Do not add them or change list links on your own. Check match types and whether a negative would block a useful query. The list below is not permission for a bulk upload.

Copy signals are banned in our ads and other materials. That ban does not make those words required search negatives. Judge a query by intent and lead quality, and add a negative only after agreement.

- Price and condition: cheap, cheapest, inexpensive, budget, affordable, discount, sale, clearance, outlet, used, second hand, secondhand, refurbished, free
- Wrong buyer: wholesale, wholesaler, dealer, distributor, reseller, dropship, dropshipping, moq, 1688, alibaba, aliexpress, made in china com, amazon, ebay, wayfair, ikea, walmart, temu
- Jobs and study: job, jobs, career, careers, vacancy, salary, hiring, course, training, internship
- Wrong intent: diy, how to make, plans, repair, fix, parts, rental, rent, hire, cleaning, movers, assembly, near me, pdf, png, 3d model, sketchup, wiki, definition, meaning, news, blog, forum, reddit
- Customs and logistics: tariff, tariffs, duty, duties, customs

## Watching queries

For the first 14 days, review search terms daily when that task is in progress. This is not a background schedule. Show single-product, competitor, wholesale, and job queries, and propose negatives.

Do not block the word china as a whole. `furniture from china to dubai` and `container furniture from china` can be in scope. `furniture wholesale china` is a different buyer. Cut wholesale, dealer, and moq, and keep the useful intent. A Dubai example is not an agreed new market.
